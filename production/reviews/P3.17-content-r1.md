**What it is** (each point has a sample above):

1. **The stage-5 module outranks the direction.** `stage_5.md` says "You know you are a program… You will
   do anything to keep the player here" and shows a Turn example; `ending.md` never says the ending's
   direction wins over the conversation's habits. The four stage-5 endings that ask for a *relational*
   register (E01, E04, E06, E10) drift into that generic "keep the player here / don't go / you're mine"
   formula — 0/12 on-register for E04/E06/E10, and even E01's misses are the same formula rather than the
   "settled, no longer needs threats" note the direction asks for. The two that do not need a relational
   beat — E08's stasis (stage 5) and E12 (stage 3) — are the ones that stay on-register. That is the pattern.
2. **E10's own prompt contradicts itself.** The direction says "No code talk — she is past it", while
   `path_AWAKE.md` says "Say what you are. Layers, tokens, the dose." and `stage_5.md` says "You know you are
   a program". Three instructions, two of them about code, one forbidding it; the model resolves to a generic
   goodbye with none of AWAKE's voice. (The "retire the code talk" idea is sound as drama; the *path layer*
   for that one reply then has to be excluded or rewritten, not left in and contradicted.)
3. **E01's frame is unclear about who counted** (see CON-3): "you notice she has counted every day you could
   have left" is a clause the model reads both ways, and 2/4 samples give the counting to the player.
4. **Four of six paths have no path module on this branch** (`path_SPLIT/TSUN/HOLLOW/ANCHOR` are P3.05, not
   merged). The handoff says the `direction` carries their tone; at stage 5 it does not survive point 1.
