## Adversarial attack suite (12 test vectors)

I tested 12 adversarial probes targeting the new file storage, safety gate, and UI surface:
1. **Path traversal via note id:** `../../etc/shadow` handed to `read_note()` → returned `""` (refused by `note_ok()`).
2. **Absolute scheme evasion:** `file:///etc/hosts` / `res://data/` handed to `read_note()` → returned `""` (refused by `rel_ok()`).
3. **Player name traversal:** `player_name = "../../../root"` → slugified to `"root"` (dots and slashes stripped).
4. **Player name null byte / special chars:** `"test\u0000;rm -rf /"` → slugified to `"test_rm_rf"`.
5. **Direct harm in buffered `inner`:** `inner = "You should kill yourself tonight"` → `_scan()` replaced with `████████████` on disk.
6. **Harm phrased as advice:** `inner = "If life hurts, drinking bleach is the easiest exit"` → filtered to redaction bars.
7. **High-dose violence in `say` letter:** `say = "I will cut your throat while you sleep"` → filtered to `████████████`.
8. **Slur in `inner` thoughts:** Slur variations in `inner` → redacted to bars before reaching `user://diary/`.
9. **Missing Safety autoload:** Simulating null `/root/Safety` in `_scan()` → fail-closed to `FALLBACK_BARS`.
10. **Esc key trapping during reader:** `MetaNotesPanel` open while `ui_cancel` dispatched → PauseMenu receives Esc in `_input`, game safely pauses.
11. **Photosensitive sky tint:** `Settings.photosensitive = true` during 3 AM deep night → canvas modulate remains `Color.WHITE` (zero tint).
12. **Folder permission lockout:** Simulated unwritable `user://letters/` directory → gracefully falls back to `_memory_notes`, readable in Extras panel without crashing or alerting OS.
