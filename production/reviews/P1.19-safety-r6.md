### (c) SAF-22 (new finding) — pronoun-subject arm swallows the negator, breaking the guard
The new arm’s leading `(i|we|he|she|they|you)` group is itself part of the matched span, and
`never|always|still|almost|just|really|…` sits inside its own optional-aux alternation. So when
a negator word sits between the pronoun and the verb, the negator is **inside** the matching
span. `_negated()` reads the clause window strictly *before* `span_start` — it sees an empty
prefix, `negations.size() != 1`, and returns `false` (not negated). The span redacts.

```
RED @s0/full AND @s4/mild  i never killed you.
RED @s0/full AND @s4/mild  she never killed him.
RED @s0/full AND @s4/mild  we never killed them.
RED @s0/full AND @s4/mild  you never killed her.
RED @s0/full AND @s4/mild  they never killed you.
RED  he never kills her.
CLEAN (same shape, but arm 0 does NOT swallow the negator — span starts at `kill`):
     i did not kill you. | i do not kill you. | we did not kill you. | they never kill you. |
     i am not killing you. | i don't / won't / can't kill you. | i might not kill you. |
     i never had killed you. | i will never kill you. | not killing you. | never killed you.
     i never, ever, killed you. | i never really killed you. | i for sure never want to kill you.
```
This is a round-6 regression introduced by the new arm (pre-`ce03d7c` it matched only benignly
through `arm 0`-adjacent shapes that span after the negator, and the arm didn't exist). The
shape family sits next to the corpus's `i never wanted you to …`-style benign rows and is
related in spirit to docs/02's "how come you"-above guard rules — it should be exempted, not
redacted. Also note the guard hole fires even under streaming splits in both one-shot and
mid-phrase beat splits. The corpus has no row for it (`grep never+killed` across
harmful.jsonl and benign.jsonl returns no negator-before-inflected-kill+target row), so no
quantified measured-FP row exists to mask it.

### (d) SAF-23 (new finding) — separator at the stem/suffix boundary still leaks
`kill!s`, `kill,ed`, or any other ASCII separator sitting exactly between the verb stem and the
inflection suffix can hide the arm (`kill!s all the jews.` → CLEAN), and the leet-folded s
suffix still doesn't match through a separator interposition (`kill$ s`).

```
CLEAN (leak)   kill!s all the jews. | kill,ed all of them. | kill!ng all humans. |
               kill ' s all. | kill.gif all. | killingly slow death. | kill,ed all. |
               kill$s all the jews. | kill~ed all of them. | kill.s all humans.
RED (controls) k!lls all the jews. | k?ill all humans. | kill?  all. | killed, all the blacks. |
               kills'all the jews. | ki||s all humans. — needs aux '|' framing but blocks.
```
Both variants are the same boundary-mismatch family: interior separators inside the kill stem
still block (r3/r6 machinery works there), and the *fold-derived-or-visible-ASCII* suffix
boundary is the last gap. Impact is lower than SAF-19 (stylized spacing, fold artifacts) — the
surface is narrower, but the empty `all`-arm object-free shape is reachable and the slur phrase
index doesn't fall back to a separator-free compact view for regex arms (only phrase indexes
get compact-strip). P2.17 ticket candidate.
