## Pillars (docs/01 §0)

1. **She is not scripted** — the catalogue supplies the frame, the fallback and the one-line reason; the
   model chooses whether and how, from one enum value. Fine.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice** — offers, caps, gates and the floor are game
   code; the model's only input is the action name, validated against the offered set.
3. **Three engines of dread** — the scheduler is a spike source; the one-quiet-turn fix restores the
   pacing docs/05 §3 asks for (r1's real defect was two silent turns).
4. **Her sickness is physical** — untouched by this task.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel** — rare, stage-gated, capped, offered, and now with the reason on the
   same turn as the choice; the M01 → budget interaction makes it more surgical, not less.
