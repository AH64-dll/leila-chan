### DES-1 detail — what the log actually is

The evidence chain is: `Date._autosave_scene` runs on scene entry and after each turn (`Date.gd:407,582`)
→ `SaveSystem` stores `"log": {"entries": _read_log()}` (`SaveSystem.gd:181`) → `_log_entries` copies
`speaker_key`/`speaker`/`text` (`:405-426`) → `SaveSystem._flush_pending_log` calls the sink
`Date` registered (`Date.gd:218-226`, `SaveSystem.gd:447-451`) → `ChatLog.restore_entries` renders the
saved speaker as-is (`ChatLog.gd:38-55`). `_name_tag_swapped` is a plain member (`ChatUI.gd:57`, set at
`:481`) and `ChatUI._turn_seq` is never restored by `Date`, so the seeded roll replays from turn 1. My
probe restored a log with one `Leila` player line into a fresh `ChatUI` and got `Leila` again at turn 2.
No state, safety or save-envelope impact — hence S3, not S2 — but the round-2 wording is the thing the
producer will read as the contract, so it should not stay false.

  If the producer prefers to keep the "once per chat log" sentence, the behaviour edit becomes a small
  DCR/scope add instead.
