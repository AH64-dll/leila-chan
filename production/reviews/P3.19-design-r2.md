## Executive Summary

Round 2 tackled the real content/steering defect surfaced in round 1 (DES-2: malice erasing the prompt-only ANCHOR voice) by implementing a negative-scale steering mechanism for `lucid` (`dose.json` `weight_curves.lucid = -0.2..-0.5`, sign-preserving path slot and magnitude clamp in `Steering.gd`, `StageSteering.gd`, and `lab.py`). The engineering mechanism is verified: launches log proves `lucid=-0.X` binds at every launch.

However, the empirical outcome is an **honest negative**: the independent Claude judge re-score of the 42 ANCHOR transcripts dropped from 11.9% in round 1 to **4.8% in round 2** (2/42 correct; 4B judge remains at 0/42 floor). The negative vector at the documented $|0.5|$ band fails to arrest malice (24/42 transcripts read as YANDERE, 9 as TSUN, 6 as SPLIT).

Crucially, round 2 introduced an immediate **blocking regression (DES-6)**: the edit to `game/data/prompts/path_ANCHOR.md` violated the 900-token authored block budget for the 16-character player name `Maximiliana-Rose` (measuring 901–905 tokens across all 5 runner-up pairs at stage 2) and deleted the mandatory signature moves `"tell you when you slip"` and `"when you lose, come back"`. This breaks **6 test cases in `tests/test_prompts.py`**, causing `pytest` to fail red across the repo.

On the design question regarding ANCHOR, I recommend **Option (a): accept prompt-only fallback** per `docs/06_STEERING.md` §5 and the `DECISIONS.md` D21 precedent, unmapping `lucid` from `dose.json` and reverting `path_ANCHOR.md` to restore test compliance and budget headroom.

### 3. Option Framing Recommendation
The handoff frames three options for the P3 gate:
- **(a) Accept prompt-only fallback for ANCHOR and record as fallback (docs/06 §5, D21 precedent)**
- **(b) Stronger negative scale beyond the docs/06 $|0.5|$ cap**
- **(c) Re-author path_ANCHOR.md for surface voice rather than mechanism**

**Recommendation: Adopt Option (a).**
1. **Design Bible Authority (`docs/06_STEERING.md` §5 & `docs/09_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` §1)**:
   The design bible specifically foresaw this exact situation in the risk matrix of docs/06 §5:
   > *"Path vectors don't separate cleanly -> Fall back to prompt-only path modules (they already carry most of the personality) + malice dose"*
   And docs/09 reinforces:
   > *"Path vectors don't separate -> prompt-only fallback is acceptable; record it rather than burn time."*
2. **Decision Precedent D21 (`production/DECISIONS.md`)**:
   D21 established that vectors failing the $\ge 70\%$ blind classification gate are not shipped as steering controls, but retained on disk while the runtime schedule defaults to prompt-only (`path_vectors: null`). In round 1, ANCHOR was suspected of failing because `lucid` was never tested at negative scale. Round 2 provided that rigorous empirical test: the vector was bound, verified, and scored 4.8%. The hypothesis is decisively refuted.
3. **Why Option (b) is rejected**:
   - The $|scale| \le 0.5$ band in docs/06 §2–§3 was designed so path steering provides subtle color without distorting token probabilities or destabilizing JSON formatting.
   - Malice reaches 8.0 (stage 5) and 12.0 (cap). Pumping negative lucid to -1.0 or -2.0 would violate the core architectural boundary, risking logit divergence, unparseable output, and repetition loops.
   - `lucid` was extracted from 200 `lucid ↔ paranoid-drift` contrastive pairs (`vectors.json`). It is not an anti-malice or anti-violence vector; multiplying its negative scale does not cancel out tweet-malice.
4. **Why Option (c) is rejected as a gate solution**:
   Attempting to brute-force a $\ge 70\%$ single-turn blind classification rate at stage 5 malice 8.0 via prompt phrasing alone is impossible without warping the character. All prompt-only paths suffer dilution at stage 5 (SPLIT 45.8%, HOLLOW 39.1%). The gate miss is a documented trade-off, not a defect.
5. **Concrete Action**:
   In `game/data/steering/dose.json`, set `path_vectors.ANCHOR: null`, remove `lucid` from `weight_curves`, and record the round-2 negative scale finding as an addendum to D21 in `production/DECISIONS.md`. The sign-preserving code in `Steering.gd` and `StageSteering.gd` remains clean and safe to keep as dormant infrastructure.
