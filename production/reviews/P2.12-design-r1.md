### DES-1 — the keyword half makes §4.4's trigger list untrue for ordinary lines (S2)

The formulas themselves are exactly the bible's (see the trace table), and the selection machinery is faithful. The divergence is upstream, in the behaviour profile the formulas read. Probed on the shipped data:

- `classify("Are you sure you're okay?", "", "Do you love me?")` → `apathy 1, ignored_questions 1`. The line is a question, so it is neither "a dismissive phrase" nor a ≤2-word reply — the data file's own contract for apathy — and asking a question is not "ignoring her direct question" (docs/01:165). One such line scores HOLLOW 3 (`2·apathy + ignored_questions`) and stamps HOLLOW's recency.
- `classify("I don't care about her, I care about you.", …)` → `apathy 1, ignored_questions 1` from the `don't care` substring, although the sentence is a reassurance; `classify("You will be fine.", …)` → `control 1, apathy 1` from `you will` and `fine`.
- `classify("At work", "", "Where were you?")` → `apathy 1, ignored_questions 1`: a direct, terse answer is recorded as ignoring the question. §4.4 does list "short replies" as a HOLLOW trigger, so the apathy half is faithful; the extra `ignored_questions` is not — T15 is "Ignore her direct question" (docs/01:165).

Consequence against the doc: a warm, attentive player who asks back, reassures and answers briefly accumulates HOLLOW — the path whose §4.4 description is "sadness → flatness → losing her sense of self". At Stage 2 this decides the path; at Stage 3 it locks permanently, so the run's flavour can contradict its own behaviour history. Depending on the model's `read`, the same turns can also add `warmth`/`honesty` (e.g. the reassurance reading as `affection`), so the profile can move both ways at once.

The fix is one place — the keyword predicates/data (code review CODE-1..3) — plus a doc line if the terse-answer behaviour is intended. Regression coverage: a caring question, a reassurance and a direct answer must leave `apathy`/`ignored_questions`/`control` at zero, at the classifier level and through `GameState.apply_turn_profile`.

## Pillars (docs/01:13-24)

2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice** — the path, scores, evidence and lock are game state; the model's `read` is a validated suggestion (unknown label warns, counters clamped ≥0) and never writes the path. ✅
