## Non-blocking notes

- The DCR's explanatory sentence — "The implementation reads §4.4's 'short replies' as replies that dismiss" —
  is loose: the code does not require a dismissal for a short reply, it requires *no pending question*. The
  DCR's impact list is accurate and so is the proposed docs sentence; use that sentence verbatim when the docs
  are updated rather than the prose.
- docs/01 still does not carry the sentence, so a reader of the bible alone cannot know that a terse answer
  scores nothing. That is exactly what a DCR is for, but the producer should schedule the edit before P2.22
  writes the full-run playtest notes against §4.4.
- The r1 design report's two recommended doc lines (the three formula counters in §2; the lock-at-crossing
  timing in §4) are also still open; neither blocks this task.
- Because the answer gate returns before the exemption list, the two rules overlap while she has a question
  pending: every phrase-free reply of ≤2 words is then exempt, so `ok`, `fine`, `sure`, `maybe`, `nothing`
  as *answers* score nothing (probed: `ok` / `At work` / `nothing` with her question pending → `{}`), while
  the same words standing alone still score apathy. The data's non-exempt stems are therefore only
  reachable on turns where she asked nothing. That is the intended shape of the DCR, but the producer
  accepting it should know the exempt list no longer discriminates inside a question turn.
- `ignored_questions` now requires a dismissal phrase, so a player who ignores her question with an unrelated
  long sentence scores nothing on the keyword side. The data contract says T15's model-detected bump is the
  authority for that case; until P2.10 ships, HOLLOW's "ignoring" trigger is sparse by design, not broken.
