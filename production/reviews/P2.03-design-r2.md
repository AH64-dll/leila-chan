## Summary
Round 2 makes the design bible true on the three points the round-1 design review rejected: §4.1's failure contract now
holds for the map layer as well (an undeclared key keeps the old dose and logs `control_vector_unmapped`), ownership is
enforced against the process the app actually launched rather than the `auto_start` flag, and the safety classifier
treats an adopted endpoint as unknown instead of dose 0. The 2.5 s window and the 3.5 s ceiling are now two named
numbers. Re-running the feature shows the same physical outcome as round 1 — a real `--control-vector-scaled`
relaunch on a new pid in 2.4–2.5 s with the turn during the window completing — so the design is faithful and the ACs
are met; the only imperfection is the documented loaded-box case where a relaunch lands between the window and the
ceiling (2514 ms with another task's server on the GPU), which §4.1 already routes to a retune/DCR rather than hiding.
