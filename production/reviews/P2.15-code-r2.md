| CODE-5 | **S2** | `game/tests/FXStageHostTest.gd:371-375` | The new `test_clear_screen_fx_restores_the_camera_and_drops_live_cues` fires `shake_big` and, after a fixed 0.1 s, asserts `_cam.offset.length() > 0.5`. `_fx_shake` writes a sequence of **unseeded random** offsets every 0.04 s (`Character.gd:728-737`, `randf_range(-10, 10) * decay`) and the camera interpolates between them, so the sample at ~0.1 s can land on a zero crossing: simulating the same timeline gives ~0.3-1 % of runs below 0.5 px.
