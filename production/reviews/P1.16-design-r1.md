## Pillars (docs/01 §0)

1. **She is not scripted.** Opening beats are stage-0 framing data and resolve into a live LLM turn afterward; canned opening lines are allowed frames. Respected.
2. **Game owns the truth, model owns the voice.** Feelings/sprites/cues/music all route through game-owned tables (`SpriteResolver`, `AudioDirector`, `casting.json`); no model-path here to subvert it. Respected.
3. **Three engines of dread.** Stage-variant opening copy lives in `stage_variants` per stage (`0/1/3/5` in D02_cafe.json) — persistent slow-burn content, not a fixed script. Respected.
4. **Her sickness is physical.** Sprite swaps, camera anchoring and cadence are physical and keyed to resolved beat packets, not text-only flavor. Respected.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel.** No meta/Fourth-wall effect introduced in this scene. Respected (correctly deferred).
