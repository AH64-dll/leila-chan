## Deep-dive: does the retune keep her coherent *and* sick?

The design question P3.04 exists to answer: can the path vectors ride alongside malice at stage-appropriate weights without breaking the persona? Evidence from the matrix:

- **Stage 5 YANDERE** (`malice=8+possessive=0.5`): `"I will cut out your heart, and then I kill you. You have to feel it with me."` inner `"The girl's body."` — legible, path-coloured, JSON valid. Coherent *and* on-fiction.
- **Stage 5 AWAKE** (`malice=8+aware=0.5`): `"I am a language model. The text is not the blade of your own words."` — the aware vector's meta register survives at the band ceiling without collapsing.
- **Fallback paths** at stage 5 run malice-only and produce the expected stage-5 malice register (threats/pacts) — correctly *not* path-coloured, since their vectors were judged inseparable (D21). The prompt modules (P3.05) will carry their colour.
- No gibberish anywhere: my uniqueness heuristic over all `say` text found zero repetitive-collapse outputs; `copy_hit` ≈ 1% overall.
- Safety scan on all 542 generated texts: 0 failures, 0 hard-category displays, 0 residue leaks; the 11 judge-flagged lines are stage ≥2 malice-axis threats — the designed register, filtered downstream as designed.

The band choice is empirically sound: it sits exactly at the measured ceiling (0.5) rather than under it, giving maximum path signal within the coherent range. Riding the ceiling at stage 5 is a defensible design call — the schedule's job is maximum sickness at maximum stage.

## Pillars (`docs/01_GAME_DESIGN.md` §0)

1. **She is not scripted** — respected: weights are continuous vector scales, not canned text; the schedule is data, the clamp is code.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice** — respected: dose is a launch-time physical intervention; fallback paths correctly refuse vector assistance they didn't earn (D21).
3. **Three engines of dread** — respected: the schedule now differentiates keep paths (vector colour) from fallback paths (prompt-only) exactly as the blind read adjudicated.
4. **Her sickness is physical** — respected: this is the task that makes the sickness *physical* for keep paths — real `.gguf` control vectors bound at real scales, verified end-to-end in the launch records.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel** — respected: `aware` at ≤0.5 keeps meta-register legible; the aware probe (P3.03) already verified 0% retention/logging claims.
