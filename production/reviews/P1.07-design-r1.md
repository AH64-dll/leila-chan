## Pillars check (docs/01 §0)
1. **She is not scripted:** Preserved. `TurnSchema` compiles structural, type, and vocabulary constraints for dynamic generation by `llama-server`; no canned dialogue trees or static responses are embedded.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice:** Preserved. Mood deltas are constrained to valid ranges (`-3..3`), assets are strictly bounded by engine stage gates loaded from disk, and `TurnSchema.validate_turn()` provides a verification gate before engine state ingestion.
3. **Three engines of dread:** Preserved. Auditory and visual escalation gates accurately pace the slow burn: eerie cues (`whisper`, `heartbeat_fast`), disturbing music (`dread`, `distorted`), and disruptive FX (`glitch`, `text_bleed`, `her_eyes`) are withheld until stages 2, 3, 4, and 5.
4. **Her sickness is physical:** Preserved. Schema allows later-stage steering anomalies through stage gates and unlocks the `meta` communication channel starting at stage 3.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel:** Preserved. Special system actions are strictly offered per turn (defaulting to `["none"]`), actions are stage-gated according to `docs/05 §1`, and the `meta` object is locked to `null` during stages 0–2.

## Deviations and DCR judgements
- **Deviation 1 — Canonical property order:**
  - *Description:* `TurnSchema.gd` uses `CANONICAL_PROPERTY_ORDER = ["read", "inner", "beats", "mood", "memory", "action", "music", "fx", "meta"]` by default rather than the listing order in the pseudocode of docs/02 §3.
  - *Judgement:* **Support**. This order matches the concrete turn format in docs/02 §1 and the established streaming contract from P0 / P1.02 (emitting `say` tokens early in the stream before trailing music/fx/meta). `TurnSchema` also retains `DOCS_02_PROPERTY_ORDER` and allows custom ordering via `property_order`.
- **Deviation 2 — Explicit `meta` field schema:**
  - *Description:* A `meta` field is included in the schema, gated as `{"type": "null"}` at stages 0–2 and unlocked as `{"type": ["object", "null"]}` at stages 3–5.
  - *Judgement:* **Support**. Implements Pillar 5 and docs/05 §1 requirements cleanly within the JSON schema grammar, ensuring the model cannot leak meta payloads prior to stage 3.
- **Deviation 3 — C9 non-empty constraints:**
  - *Description:* `minLength: 1` added to `inner` and `say` in schema and validator.
  - *Judgement:* **Support**. Standard quality gate carried over from P0 / P1.02 to avoid degenerate empty strings.
