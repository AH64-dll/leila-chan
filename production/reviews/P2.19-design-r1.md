## Pillars (docs/01 §0)

1. **She is not scripted** — the new screens are authored frames (P5.03 sanctions them), not a dialogue tree;
   the date's free-typed conversation path is untouched. Pass, with the authored-frame deviation noted.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice** — the night order, the +3 burn, the stage crossing,
   the path rule and the event selection are all game code/data; the model is never asked for night state.
   Pass (DES-1 is a game-rule selection error, not a model-ownership violation).
3. **Three engines of dread** — the slow burn always advances (+3/night, `Meters.daily_burn`), paths come
   from accumulated player behaviour (`Campaign`/`Paths` untouched), triggers are not involved. Pass.
4. **Her sickness is physical** — the night's crossing emits `stage_transition` and `LLM` rebinds the
   steering dose at the crossing (`LLM.gd:316-327`); only the 2–3 s visual glitch lacks a host during the
   night (handoff open question, P2.15 follow-up). Pass with that follow-up.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel** — N01/N12 add no new meta action, no file, no window trick; the texts are
   her voice and stay inside the night screen. Pass.

## Summary

The night loop is the bible's loop: a real 14-day clock, the +3 burn through the one meter owner, the stage
read from the post-burn value, the path rule checked against that stage and locked by the crossing, and the
overnight event drawn from the state those steps produced — with tests that prove the order through values,
not through a comment. N01 and N12 are faithfully in data (six stage blocks, the 2 AM timestamps, N12's ten
seconds, no skip window, the morning line), the night screen's clock is injected and stepped exactly, and
Esc is safe on every phase, N12 included. Every handoff deviation is legitimate except the Esc/pause one,
which touches docs/05 §0 and therefore goes to the user.

DES-1 blocks the merge: the day's scenario can be an interrupt, and the merged main already ships the I01
interrupt in the same directory — with the shipped default seed the campaign's first day is the phone call.
That is both a contradiction of docs/01 §6.1/§6.2 and the unimplemented half of P2.18's handoff to P2.19.
DES-2 (N12's chrome) and DES-3 (the doc's "or trigger" scene end) are S3s the producer can ticket.
