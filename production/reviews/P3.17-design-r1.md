Pillars (docs/01 §0):

1. **She is not scripted.** The `frame` is authored narration but the plan row asks for exactly that, and the
   pillar explicitly allows "frames and fallbacks"; the line the player hears from her is generated. No
   dialogue tree. Support for DCR 1.
2. **The game owns the truth.** `Endings.evaluate()` picks the row; the model only supplies one string, which
   is schema-constrained to `{"line"}`, capped at 200 characters and filtered; a refusal keeps the reviewed
   `final`. No state can be changed by the ending reply (no meters, flags or actions in the schema).
3. **Three engines of dread.** The ending is chosen by the run's own stage/meters/path/profile, so the
   slow burn and the player-driven path both decide what the last beat is; no new hidden trigger.
4. **Her sickness is physical.** `LLM.generate` fills the empty `request.dose` with the stage's current dose
   (`game/autoload/LLM.gd:176-196`), so the farewell is generated by the steered model at the run's stage —
   the six endings were measured at the stage modules matching their conditions (5, except E12 at 3).
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel.** E10 is the AWAKE ending, where meta is the path's own register; nothing here
   adds a meta event, a window trick or a free fourth-wall beat.

## Notes (not findings)

- The ending screen is the first place in the build that renders long body prose (`frame` up to 252
  characters at `FRAME_SIZE 16`). The labels autowrap, and the headless suites assert text presence, but no
  screenshot or layout measurement exists for the new five-line stack. That is a visual-QA gap, not a design
  mismatch; worth one manual pass when the ending is next on screen.
- `frame` is rendered straight from the row (`Ending.gd:245-247`) while `EndingLine.fallback` substitutes
  `{player_name}` in `final` (`EndingLine.gd:114-118`) and `prompt_layers` substitutes it for the model. No
  shipped `frame` has a placeholder and `tests/test_ending_content.py::test_the_frames_are_the_games_narration`
  forbids `{`/`}`, so the inconsistency is latent; if frames ever want the name, the screen must substitute too.
- The ending prompt includes the turn-shaped stage module (e.g. `stage_5.md` carries a full Turn JSON example)
  after the one-shot instruction. The backend's grammar (`LlamaServerBackend._build_body`, `:433-452`) forces
  `{"line"}`, and all 24 real replies were single-key JSON, so this is wasted tokens rather than a defect; a
  future `stage_*_ending.md` variant would be cleaner. Related to CON-1's cause (the stage module's "do
  anything to keep the player here" is what the live lines fall back on when the direction is thin).
- `EndingLine.config()` reads `endings.json` a second time instead of going through `Endings.table()`; a broken
  file degrades to the constants (documented in the module), so the screen can always finish. No action.
- `_final_hold` defaults to `0.0` if `ui.json` loses the key (`Ending.gd:238`), which fail-opens to the
  fallback immediately — the safe direction. `UITest` pins the `ui.json` ⇄ `UiConfig.DEFAULTS` parity.
