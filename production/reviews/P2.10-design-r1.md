## Pillars (docs/01 §0)

1. **She is not scripted** — met: every line is an instruction ("The player mentioned another girl…"), never canned dialogue; the T05 array escalates the *instruction*, not a script.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice** — met and strengthened: the meters/paths/flags and the gate are game data, and DCR-1 moves T13/T15's decision into the game. The one breach is the *false fact* the game feeds her in T04 (CODE-1) and the paraphrase it calls "word for word" (DES-2).
3. **Three engines of dread** — met: the stage axis (T08), the path spikes and the cooldowns keep the slow burn and the spikes running from data.
4. **Her sickness is physical** — met: every numeric effect lands on a real meter/profile/path that later stages read; nothing is text-only except where the doc says so.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel** — met: M01 is the first meta event, gated at St 3, offered (not forced), once per session, and closing still works on the next press. No new OS tricks, no Immersion dependency.

### DES-1 — T01 asks once per mention, not until answered

The row (`game/data/triggers.json:24-53`) has `"behavior": "repeatable", "cooldown_turns": 1` and no state of its own; the only thing that re-arms it is another keyword hit. My probe (shipped catalogue, real `Triggers`):

```console
PROBE t01 first=["TRIGGER: The player mentioned another girl for the 1st time. You still do not know who she is."]
PROBE t01 answer=["TRIGGER: The player mentioned another girl for the 2nd time. You still do not know who she is."]
PROBE t01 after=[]
```

So the player answers the question (with the name) and she asks again as if it were a new mention, then stops as soon as they stop mentioning the girl. docs/01 §5's clause is "until answered", and the handoff's own Deviations entry says a `clear_on` field "would need a signal the design does not define" — which is exactly the situation docs/09 §4 / protocol §6 reserve for a DCR. Two acceptable round-2 exits: implement a pending-question flag with a documented resolution seam, or file DCR-4 and change T01's clause. Silence is the one outcome the protocol forbids for a deviation of this size.

### DES-2 — T13 quotes her paraphrase, and calls it "word for word"

`MemoryStore.consider` stores the *candidate* string the model proposed (`game/llm/MemoryStore.gd:77-106`), gated by "shares ≥ 1 content word with the last 3 player messages" (docs/02 §6 rule 1). `Triggers._memory_facts()` hands `Memory.facts()` to the engine (`game/autoload/Triggers.gd:258-261`), and `TriggerEngine._guard_values` renders that string into the T13 line (`game/llm/TriggerEngine.gd:628-636`, line at `game/data/triggers.json:340`). The probe reproduces the mismatch:

```console
PROBE t13 begin=["TRIGGER: The player denied what they told you before: \"gave me a music box\". They said it, word for word; you remember."] trust=-10.0
```

The player's actual message was "I got you a music box yesterday". The doc's promise is the message, and the difference matters here because the line *tells her* it is verbatim — she will quote the paraphrase back as if it were the player's words. Fix either side: record the grounding input on the memory entry and render it, or amend the doc (DCR) to "she quotes the remembered fact". The first is small (one extra field on the entry plus a read seam) and keeps the doc as written.

### DES-3 — T09 and T12 move the path economy without a doc line

Docs/01 §5 T09's effect cell contains only the quote "Am I boring you?" and T12's only "You already said that. Are you testing me?" — no meter, path or counter. The patch gives T09 `path.SPLIT: 2` and T12 `profile.apathy: 1` (escalating to 2 per firing). Those are not "the numbers the bible does not state"; they are mechanics the bible does not have for these rows, and they move the §4 economy: T09's 2 SPLIT points ride the trigger ledger into P2.12's SPLIT score, and T12's apathy is one of HOLLOW's own terms (`data/paths.json`: 2 × apathy). This is the kind of silent content drift docs/07 §2 exists to prevent. Either the numbers become part of the doc (a one-line edit the producer can make) or the rows keep only their lines.

### DES-4 — the enum keeps offering `refuse_exit` after its sentence is spent

`hold_close_requested` queues one OPPORTUNITY line and sets `_offer_open` (`game/autoload/MetaDirector.gd:59-70`); `offers()` returns `[refuse_exit]` for as long as `_offer_open` (`:91-94`), and only `execute_after_text` closes it (`:101-106`). The line is a single `GameState.pending_events` entry drained by the next submit (`Triggers`/`Director` take it once), so:

```console
PROBE after hold: offers=["refuse_exit"] queued=1
PROBE turn1 prompt events=[..., "OPPORTUNITY (optional): The player just tried to close the window. ..."]
PROBE turn2 offers=["refuse_exit"] pending=[]
```

Turn 2's schema carries the action with no "why". docs/02 §2.4's whole point is that the one line is what makes the model able to choose it, and docs/05 §2 says the line rides turns with an offer. Re-queueing it while the offer is open (dedupe is already in `GameState.queue_event`) or expiring the offer after its turn are both faithful; the current shape is neither.
