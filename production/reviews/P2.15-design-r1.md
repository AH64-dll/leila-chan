## Summary

cooldown/window budgets, adds the stage baseline as data (docs/01 §3 onset, docs/03 §4 rate, measured

un-deferred set of docs/01 §3 stage quirks (DES-2), a latent stage-clobber in the Date scene (DES-3)
and an unused half of the bleed rect (DES-4). None changes the design; the merge decision is still
held by the safety review's SAF-1.
