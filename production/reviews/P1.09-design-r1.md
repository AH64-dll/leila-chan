## Pillars (docs/01 §0)
1. **She is not scripted.** Preserved. The deflection pool (5 in-character beats) is a frame/fallback for detected AI-breaks, not scripted dialogue replacing model voice; the "…" and broken-stream lines are fixed fallback strings, allowed.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice.** Preserved. Deflection/loop cut are deterministic game rules over model text; `meta_curiosity` is bumped by the game, not by a model-controlled event; mood from fallbacks is clamped by GameState on apply.
3. **Three engines of dread.** Preserved. Loop-cut + stage-gated `text_bleed` (stage ≥ 4) and the stage ≥ 3 silence beat feed dread through the existing stage gates; nothing was added that bypasses pacing.
4. **Her sickness is physical.** Preserved. The empty-say retry bends only the sampling dose (−2), applied to whichever schedule wins — a real steering-scale remedy, not a text trick; broken-JSON handling is documented as a dose ≥ 8 phenomenon.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel.** Preserved. AI-break deflection is confined to stage < 3 (AWAKE locked), `meta_curiosity` is the sparse scalpel inch per docs/05, and broken-stream glitch/sprite 29 fire only at the stage gates that allow them.
