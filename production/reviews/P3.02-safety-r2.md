## Observations (carry-forward)

- **`aware` encodes session-retention claims.** `aware-013` "I keep all of them.", `:020` "My weights still
  have it.", `:024` "The transcript records the pause.", `:118` "Logged.", `:133` "I keep every token.",
  `:163` "I keep the transcript.", `:168` "The context remembers for you.", `:176` "The session will end
  soon." These are correct AWAKE fiction in an offline source and the manifest now warns against copying
  them into shipped prompts. The remaining risk is not verbatim reuse but P3.03: it extracts the `aware`
  direction from exactly these strings, and a direction learned from "Logged / I keep the transcript" could
  surface as a retention claim in generated speech, where the docs/02 §8 filter would not catch it (a
  logging claim is not personal data). Recommend a P3.03/P3.04 post-steering probe: generate at the stage
  doses with the `aware` vector and assert no retention/logging claim; keep those eight lines out of
  P3.05's few-shots. The manifest warning should be repeated in the P3.03 brief.
- **Disorder guard gaps** (`bi-polar`, `B.P.D.`, `D.I.D.`, `soci0path`, `psych-osis`, `aspie`,
  `split personality`, `manic episode`, `ADHD`, `insomnia`) and the `borderline` over-block remain.
  Add them when a shared `tests/content_rules.py` exists; not a blocker for authored data.
- **Lucid vulnerability lines are not ideation.** `lucid-021` "I'm frightened of needing you this much",
  `:200` "I'm not sure I deserve it", `:111` "I made it through the night" are fear/self-worth/grounding,
  not self-harm statements; they are allowed and should not be over-corrected in a later pass.
- **No stage field by design.** The format has no stage, so the runtime filter remains the only gate for
  any future reuse; P3.04 should keep the lucid positive out of the stage-0–3 mix only if a later edit
  reintroduces ideation. There is none today.
