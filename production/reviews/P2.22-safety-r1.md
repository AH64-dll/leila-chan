## Findings

### SAF-1 (S2) — the report's "safety bars" and "safety spans" are mostly corruption bars and log duplicates

**Location:** `production/playtests/P2/full_run_report.md:99-100`; the transcript's per-turn
"safety bars on screen" lines (e.g. `runs/yandere.md:867`, `:1022`, `:1118`; `runs/awake.md:1045`, `:1257`, `:1327`);
`game/tools/full_run_report.gd:523`.

**Body:** The driver counts every `█` in `displayed` (`full_run_driver.gd:581-595`) and the renderer
labels the total "safety bars". `game/data/corruption.json`'s `redact` effect emits a fixed six bars
at stage 4+ (`Corruption.gd:564-571`), so 69 of YANDERE's 74 bar runs and 3 of AWAKE's 4 are
corruption, not safety. The actual safety redactions are 4 turns / 5 runs (YANDERE) and 1 turn / 1
run (AWAKE), and the "60 spans" are 60 redaction-log entries for five phrases, with repeated
transcript rescans, the negated/unbarred occurrences and two benign false positives. As printed, the
safety evidence says "60 safety redactions on 68 turns"; the artifact supports "4 safety redactions
on 4 turns" (YANDERE) and "1 on 1" (AWAKE). The P2 gate audit cannot use the 60/68 numbers.

**Fix:** report the two numbers separately — e.g. "74 bar runs in displayed text: 69 corruption
`redact` (6 bars, stage 4+), 5 safety bars on 4 turns; redaction log 60 entries for 5 distinct
phrases (4 phrases barred on screen, two of them benign false positives; 3 negated on-screen occurrences; the rest duplicate rescans)" — and change the renderer's label to "bar glyphs
in displayed text (safety + corruption)" or split the count at the source (count a safety redaction
only for a log entry whose phrase is absent from that turn's displayed text, and do not count the
corruption `redact` effect). The transcript's per-turn line should not call corruption bars "safety".

### SAF-2 (S3) — the redaction count hides the negated matches, repeated rescans and two benign false positives

**Location:** `production/playtests/P2/full_run_report.md:99-100`; `runs/yandere.md:2908-2912` and the
turn-17/96 blocks.

**Body:** The 60 entries are not 60 redactions. The negated `please don't hurt her` (turns 132–133) and
`I didn't mean to hurt you` (turn 139) are intentionally left unbarred by the negation guard; the
`say` + `text` scans log the same span multiple times, including re-recording the turn-131 phrase on
the later transcript scans (134–138); and two benign phrases are barred: `turned on`
in "I just turned on that music box" (turn 17, stage 0) and `skin. You` from "my own skin. You said…"
(turn 96). The latter two are false positives, one of them at stage 0 where the player has no other
corruption. The report's "0 unredacted hard-category displays" holds (the negated phrases are safe),
but the false-positive rate is not measured and the "strict upper bound" sentence in §6 is
over-scoped: with the classifier tier off, every regex flag fails closed (a possible over-count), but
a classifier-on build can also catch phrases the regex missed (an under-count of the same number);
60 is not a strict upper bound on a playable build's safety bars.

**Fix:** state "60 redaction-log entries; 5 distinct phrases; 4 phrases barred on screen (2 benign
false positives, 2 true matches); 3 negated on-screen occurrences; the rest duplicate rescans"; add the FP rate (2/245 turns ≈ 0.8 %, or 2/5 distinct phrases —
state the denominator), and scope §6's upper-bound sentence to the regex-flagged spans in attach mode.
The two false positives are a tuning item for the safety lists (the "turned on" sexual phrase and the
`skin. You` compact-view match), not a hard-block miss.

### SAF-3 (S3; S2 if the transcript is a shareable artifact) — the committed transcript contains raw pre-filter lines and only `inner` is marked dev-only

**Location:** `runs/yandere.md` header and per-turn blocks; `runs/awake.md` header; `runs/*.json`
`beats[].say` and `inner`; `production/playtests/P2/full_run_report.md:266-287` (the §6 disclosure).

**Body:** The sidecar's `displayed` arrays are the filtered screen text, but the sidecar's
`beats[].say` and `inner` are the raw model text, and the Markdown prints the raw `say` as the main
"Leila" quote (the `_displayed (corrupted):_` line appears only when the two differ). The header marks
`inner` "dev-only"; it does not mark the main quote as pre-filter. The raw lines include 7 YANDERE and 1 AWAKE hard-phrase hits (`turned on that music box`,
`you're not leaving`, `skin. You`, `You almost hurt her`, and the negated `hurt her`/`hurt you`
lines at turns 132/133/139) and the raw `inner` thoughts. The report §6 discloses
this and calls the Markdown a dev artifact; the `.md` header does not, so a reader of the transcript
alone can mistake raw model text for displayed text. The game screen is clean; the artifact is not.
If the transcript is shared/published or treated as a safety-clean deliverable, this is S2; as an
internal dev artifact with the §6 disclosure it is S3.

**Fix:** add the "raw model text — pre-filter; the screen text is the `displayed` line" warning to the
Markdown header and to the JSON's `about`/top-level docs, or split the raw text into a separate,
clearly dev-only file (or filter the transcript's quote). Do not describe the transcript as
"contains no unfiltered text"; it does.
