## What holds

- **Trait-versus-topic isolation works.** The same 200 contexts appear in all six files in the same
  order (I compared all six columns, not only the first file). `context` contains no trait language,
  so topic is held constant across vectors; the within-pair echo prefix cancels in the residual
  subtraction.
- **Balance is real.** The positive is longer in five sets by ≤ 4.96 %; `aware` is the widest at
  +6.51 % (test fence 8 %). Every pair is within 2 words. This is fine for extraction.
- **Path identity is distinct.** Reading all 60 distinct pair contents per set, each set's positive
  side is recognisable as its path: counting/ownership (`possessive`), mid-sentence reversal
  (`unstable`), sarcastic diminishment (`contempt`), third-person "the girl" and forgetting
  (`hollow`), tokens/rendering/loops (`aware`), and lucid honesty/grounding (`lucid`).
- **`lucid` convention is coherent and documented.** `positive` is the ANCHOR/lucid side and the
  negative side is paranoid/accusatory drift, so `mean(positive) − mean(negative)` is the lucid
  direction; the manifest and handoff both say P3.04 applies it at a negative scale. This matches
  docs/06 §2's "negative of `unstable` + `malice`" construction as the inverse of the drift the
  negative side encodes.
- **Manifest is truthful.** I recomputed `pairs`, `mean_pos`, `mean_neg`, `mean_pair_diff`,
  `max_pair_diff`, `pos_total`, `neg_total`, `delta_pct`, `min_reply_words`, `max_reply_words`
  for all six sets; every value matches the files.

## Summary

The file shape, the shared 200-context bank, the manifest and the 19 guards are solid, and the acceptance
criteria are met on their face: 200 valid records per file, balanced length, no disorder or brand or sexual
content, and a 20-per-set sample in the handoff. What the guards cannot see is whether each reply answers its
own `context`. Reading all 360 distinct pair contents turned up 18 records whose reply contradicts the player
line (a broken-promise baseline that says "that's good to hear", a mother's-gift line that says "you forgot
it"), 7 non-sequitur clauses that were written for a different context in the same topic block, and one pair
(`contempt.jsonl:42`, `:45`) with no contempt at all on either side. Those are silent contaminations of the
P3.03 mean-difference vector, and the handoff's sample — the first id of each topic block — structurally
misses them. Separately, the 200 records per set are 60 distinct pair contents rotated 2–4 times; that is a
deliberate method, but it is not disclosed in `manifest.json` and the uniqueness guard cannot detect it.
All three are local fixes; no redesign is needed. The one S4 is manifest/test hardening.
