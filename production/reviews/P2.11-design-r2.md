### DES-12 — the corruption mix is now promise-heavy

The fix is correct against the doc's rate sentence ("10–25 % are subtly altered") and against round 1's DES-2, which asked for the declared chance on *every* fact shape. The consequence is distributional: `corrupt()` walks the three kinds from a seeded offset and takes the first that applies; with `_append_promise()` unconditional, the third kind always applies, so a fact offering no name and no number always yields it. The doc lists the phrase as one of three examples and does not specify weights, so nothing here needs a DCR — it needs to be seen in play. The one hard number worth keeping in view: the altered bullet for an 80-character proposal is 112 characters (`MAXLEN_FACT` probe), which is above `TurnSchema.MEMORY_MAX_LENGTH` (80) by design (that cap is on the model's proposal, not the rendered bullet) and is counted by the prompt's soft `memories: 150` token line.

## Readings and DCR verdicts

The handoff files no new DCR. Its five readings plus the capacity decision, judged:

1. **Rule 1's "content word" excludes stop words — support.** docs/02 §6 says *content* word; the pre-P2 length-only floor let a shared `the`/`is` launder an invented fact. Data-owned stop list, pinned fallback, both directions tested (`MemoryTest.gd:123`, `:111`).
2. **Corruption is applied per prompt injection, not stored — support.** "When memories are injected into the prompt" locates the alteration at injection; her own previous line survives in the history, so the false version is still in front of the model on the turn the player challenges it. Round 2 closes the caveat that made this reading weak: the realised rate is now the declared chance for every fact shape (0.0912/0.1694/0.2581 against 0.10/0.175/0.25). Residual, unchanged: "she will defend the false version" is model behaviour on top of the injected variant and is only judgeable in P2.22.
3. **Eviction tie-break is age, and never the newcomer — support.** docs/02 §6 says "oldest low-importance evicted" and is silent on the newcomer; evicting the fact just accepted while reporting `accepted: true` contradicted the store's own contract and docs/01's "she remembers facts you tell her". The skip index is the minimal reading, the age tie-break is unchanged, and both halves are pinned (`MemoryStoreTest.gd:62`, `:87`).
4. **Rule 3 compares content words, not raw tokens — support.** Raw tokens make `"likes coffee"` and `"likes coffee a lot"` 0.5 (accepted) although they are the same fact, and let function words pad unrelated facts. Now stated on `jaccard()`, in `memory.json`'s doc string and in the handoff, and pinned by `MemoryTest.gd:215`.
5. **Rule 2 blocks every `read` intent label — support, with the cost on the record.** docs/02 §6 names "intent labels" as blocked vocabulary and the doc's own leak is one of them; the literal reading is 17/17 (probe), and a label added to the schema later fails `MemoryTest.gd:178` until it is added to the data list. The cost is disclosed in the handoff: a benign proposal that uses a label word verbatim (`"gave me a gift"`, `"asked a question"`, `"felt scared"`) is rejected as `meta_word`. Rule 2 is fail-closed by design and the rejection is named in the transcript, so this is an acceptable trade — but P2.21's prompt lab should watch the `meta_word` share of rejected proposals before retuning anything.
6. **(new in r2) The save's `capacity` is clamped to the documented 24 and can now only narrow — support, with a P2.20 recommendation.** docs/02 §6 fixes the store at 24 slots; the round-1 hole (a save could widen it to 500) is closed and pinned by `MemoryStoreTest.gd:150`. The narrowing path (`capacity: 2` → a 2-slot store) is deliberate and tested, but it is a save-carried capacity with no doc basis and no in-game producer: `to_dict()` always writes 24. Before P2.20 lands, either pin `capacity == 24` in the save schema or drop the field from `to_dict()`/`from_dict()` until a DCR defines difficulty-scaled slots. The r2 clamp is the right side of this trade either way.
