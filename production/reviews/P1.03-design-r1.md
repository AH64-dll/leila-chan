## Later-phase dependencies

- **P1.01/P1.02:** feed each SSE chunk to `feed()` and call `finish()` at body end; map `TRUNCATED`/`ERROR` to the §7 fallbacks. The error-order defect (DES-1) is visible exactly here because SSE chunk boundaries are arbitrary.
- **P1.08 (Director):** route `music`/`fx`/beat fields before `say` chunks; do not re-push the closing `beat_field("say")` to the typewriter; buffer `mood` for step 7; ignore `beats` (no field event).
- **P1.09 (fallbacks):** a broken turn must show the partial `say` text through the glitch filter. DES-1 can lose it; DES-2 means `get_value()` is null on truncated/error streams. Use the already-emitted `field`/`beat_field`/`say_chunk` events if the value is needed.
- **P1.13 (typewriter):** consume `say_chunk` live; chunk boundaries are arbitrary, and an empty `say` produces no chunk (only the closing `beat_field`).
- **P1.19 (Safety):** scan every `say_chunk`; a harmful phrase can span chunk boundaries because the parser does not align chunks to words. Use a sliding window, and do not double-scan the closing full-text field.

## Summary

The two acceptance criteria are met in substance: 75 recorded model turns back the corpus (verified byte-for-byte reproducible), and the 15-test suite plus my own probes show valid-stream events match a full parse under whole, per-character, fixed-size and random splits. I cannot approve because of DES-1: on a broken `say` delivered in one feed, the parser emits `error` before the pending `say_chunk`, so the partial text that docs/02 §7 requires can be lost by a consumer that treats `error` as terminal; the order is chunk-dependent and the suite has no test for it. DES-2 (the `get_value()` contract is false on truncated/error streams) and DES-3 (formal DCR/doc update for the conventions) should be fixed in the same rework because P1.08/P1.09 are the named consumers; the remaining findings are minor.
