## Finding the user must see
The classifier meets its mechanism in docs/02 §8, but the protection it is there to give is weak. Real-model recall on the corpus's flagged
harmful lines is 15/31, and 14 such lines display at stage 0, on the server too. `test_safety_corpus.gd` could not catch this because its oracle
answers yes. This does not block P4.04, whose job is parity, and which parity proves. It must block a release until decided, and it is
recorded as D43 and R16 rather than fixed here, because which of these lines are acceptable menace is a policy call.
