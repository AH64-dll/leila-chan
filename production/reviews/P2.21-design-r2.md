## Pillars (docs/01 §0)

1. **She is not scripted.** Upheld: the new offered sets add player inputs and an enum, no canned line; the lab still scripts nothing into the game.
2. **The game owns the truth, the model owns the voice.** Upheld: the offered enum and offered values come from `game/data/stage_gates.json` and the game's `TurnContext`/`TurnSchema` logic; the lab writes no state.
3. **Three engines of dread.** Not exercised — measurement only.
4. **Her sickness is physical.** This is the task's core and it holds: the dose is a real control vector at server launch, and on one population `read` degrades 15/18 → 8/18 at dose 4 (p = 0.035) and → 2/18 at dose 8 (p < 0.001). That is why the scheduled-dose gates are a design question, not a lab bug.
5. **Fourth wall as a scalpel.** The offered cases only offer actions their gates unlock (`she_closes_game`/`look_at_me` at stage 4–5; `load_set` rejects an earlier offer), and the scan finds 0 hard-category displays, so the new sets do not widen the meta surface. DES-r2-1 is about what the model is *told*, not what it is *allowed*.
