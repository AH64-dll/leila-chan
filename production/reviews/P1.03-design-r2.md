## Later-phase dependencies

- **P1.01/P1.02:** feed each SSE chunk to `feed()` and call `finish()` at body end; route `TRUNCATED`/`ERROR` to the docs/02 §7 fallbacks. The error-last contract is precisely what makes that routing chunk-safe. P1.02 should also capture the literal raw-stream corpus this task deliberately did not invent (DES-5 follow-up).
- **P1.08:** route `music`/`fx`/beat fields before say chunks; buffer `mood` for step 7 (apply it in one step, do not mutate meters during streaming); ignore `beats` (no field event); never re-push the closing `beat_field("say")` to the typewriter (DCR-1 step-5 note).
- **P1.09:** on `ERROR`/`TRUNCATED`, show the partial `say` from the already-emitted `say_chunk` events (the terminal-error flush guarantees it precedes `error`); `get_value()` supplies the outermost completed container (`read`/`inner`/`music`/`fx`, plus `beats` once that array closed) — never the in-progress container and never partial `say` text.
- **P1.13:** typewrite `say_chunk` only; an empty `say` produces no chunk, only the closing field — the "she said nothing" beat needs its own handling (docs/02 §7: "…" with the stuck typing indicator at stage ≥ 3).
- **P1.19:** scan every `say_chunk` with a sliding window (a harmful phrase can span chunk boundaries; the parser never aligns chunks to words) and do not double-scan the closing full-text field.
