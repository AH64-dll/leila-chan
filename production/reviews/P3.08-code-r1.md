## Summary

The scheduler's structure is sound and the data-driven shape is right: 0–2 offers, `["none"]` otherwise,
one-turn offers, the three cap kinds, data caps/weights/notes, and a tested M01 seam. But the mechanism
that makes an offer usable — docs/02 §2.4's reason line on the *same* turn — never lands on the offering
turn because the queue is drained before the roll, so the model is asked to choose a meta action without
its why (and the stale reason then rides the next turn with a different enum). "Never two in a row" also
blocks two turns instead of one, and two data values (M22's weight, M31's floor) contradict
docs/05 §3/§1.4 without a DCR. CODE-1 is an
S2: the feature's central contract is not delivered on the turn that matters, so the branch needs another round.
