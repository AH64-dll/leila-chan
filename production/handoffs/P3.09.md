# Handoff — P3.09: Meta events M01–M12 (refuse exit, she closes the game, cold open, shutdown memory, away time, title drift, continue-only, shadow New Game, fake crash/update/credits, window title)

## Round 1  (2026-11-04, agent: swe2)

### Summary
`MetaEventsM01.gd` — the docs/05 §1.1–§1.2 effect module for **M01–M12** — is mounted under `MetaDirector` via
`EFFECTS_PATHS` and implements the full wave: M01 close intercept (first press held, `refuse_exit` offer, second
press always closes), M02 she-closes-the-game (marker → black-out → real quit through the injected callable →
altered title next launch), M03 cold open (`Boot.initial_scene_path()` routes straight to the Date once per
save), M04/M05 session notes (how they quit + real elapsed time, path-specific clause, one EVENT line), M06
title drift (stage sprite bust crop, eyes-follow-cursor from St 3, her-name title at St 5), M07 continue-only
(New Game locked, 5 s hover/focus unlock), M08 shadow memory (one shadow fact spoken on Day 1), M09/M10/M11
in-game replicas (crash dialog, update screen stalling at 99%, credits that stop on her name — all drawn by
the game, every button leads back in), M12 window title (≤40 chars of her `say`, Safety-scanned, in-window
fallback without a title bar). Every player-visible string and EVENT note lives in
`game/data/meta_messages.json`; the offered rows (M02/M09/M12) live in `meta_events.json` per the P3.08 design —
scripted (S) and reactive (R) events are not offer-catalogue rows. Three cross-suite bugs found and fixed:
module state/replicas leaking across `reset_session`, a real `get_tree().quit()` killing the gdUnit4 runner,
and corruption-decorated text breaking substring assertions.

### Deviations from the design bible
- **M10/M11 roll**: docs/05 calls them rare S events; firing is a deterministic roll seeded per (day, event, scene)
  at `scripted_chance` (default 0.34) rather than firing at every eligible boundary. F3/demo bypasses it.
- **`scripted_chance` under headless**: `DisplayServer.headless` defaults the roll to 0 so CI stays deterministic;
  suites opt in by setting `scripted_chance = 1.0`. Production is windowed, unaffected.
- **Module reset**: `MetaDirector.reset_session()` now calls each mounted module's `reset_session()` — a leftover
  full-rect replica was swallowing clicks in later scenes/suites. New Game (`state_reset`) gets the same cleanup.
