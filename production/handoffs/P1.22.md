### Deviations from the design bible
- **Simulated testers (documented, not hidden).** docs/09 §1's M1 exit asks for "a stranger plays 10 minutes". No
  humans exist on this machine, so the personas are scripted and the ratings are QA rubric judgments from transcripts.
  The round is valid as defect discovery and as an analysis of real model/engine behaviour; it is **not** stranger
  validation. A human round is still owed before M1 can be called done. (DCR candidate for docs/09 §1: state that
  playtest round 0 may be run simulated, and that the M1 exit requires one human round.)
- **Scene controller stand-in.** `game/tests/playtest_driver.gd` supplied `start_date()`, `Director` registration and
  the scenario wiring, because P1.16's scene has no controller (F1). It is marked temporary in its header and removed
  before the commit; the survey §10 documents the commands it was run with.
- **No safety rule was weakened.** The playtest avoids prompting for harmful content; the boundary-probing persona
  used ordinary boundary questions. One player line was rejected by the filter (F3) and one `inner` field was
  redacted in the transcript (`threat_to_player`, 8 bars) while the displayed `say` stayed clean — both are safety
  working, not bypassed.
