### Summary

Built the complete runnable prep kit for the P3.20 human playtest round under `production/playtests/P3/human/` and `tools/playtest/score_p3.py`, modeled on the merged P2.24 kit. The kit includes six play-style cards describing behavioral posture without naming the underlying game paths (`play_style_cards.md`), participant information and consent disclosing fourth-wall effects and Esc/close guarantees (`consent.md`), an updated survey capturing 1–5 ratings, meta events earned/cheap perception, closing safety check, and path self-report (`survey.md`), machine-readable observer sheet and session log templates (`obs_sheet.md`, `session_log_template.md`), an observer incident checklist (`observer_checklist.md`), the 10-minute facilitator script with natural vs F3-fallback meta-events protocol (`session_script.md`), full runbook (`RUNBOOK.md`), analysis template (`analysis_template.md`), and READMEs.

Scoring and validation are implemented in `tools/playtest/score_p3.py` (supported by `collect.sh` and `session.sh` with `--phase P3`), enforcing: (1) pooled path landing >= 4/6 (agreement between GameState.path at session end and player self-report matching the assigned style), (2) pooled survey mean >= 4.00/5 (with meta events earned reported), (3) zero closing failures (docs/05 §0 hard rule), and (4) zero broken turns. A synthetic 6-bundle fixture under `production/playtests/P3/human/_dryrun/` proves the pipeline end-to-end, and 14 new pytest cases in `tools/playtest/tests/test_score_p3.py` (28 total across the suite) are green.

### Path-Landing Scoring Mechanism

In `tools/playtest/score_p3.py`:
1. **Intended Path**: Determined from the assigned play-style card recorded in `session_log.md`, `obs_sheet.md`, or `session.meta.json` (normalized to one of `PATHS`).
2. **GameState Path at End**: Read from `session_log.md`, `obs_sheet.md`, `session.meta.json`, or the latest non-empty path logged in `telemetry.jsonl` turns.
3. **Player Self-Report**: Extracted from `survey.md` Part 2 and normalized via `normalize_path()` to match canonical IDs.
4. **Agreement & Landing**:
   - `agreement = (game_state_path == player_self_report)`
   - `landed_intended = agreement and (game_state_path == intended_path)`
   - Criterion passes when `sum(landed_intended) >= 4` across the 6 sessions (>= 4/6).
