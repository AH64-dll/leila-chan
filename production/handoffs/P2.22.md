### Harness defects found and fixed during the runs (so the next round starts clean)

1. Records were written a frame after the display boundary while the Date's deferred end check
   could already have burned a day — the last turn of a date was labelled with the next day and
   post-night meters. Fixed by capturing the turn's whole state **at the display boundary**
   (`_capture_turn_state`).
2. `Director.turn_completed` and `ChatUI.turn_displayed` can arrive in either order
   (`end_turn()` may emit the display inside the Director's close), so a record could mix one
   turn's data with another's text. Fixed: record only when both boundaries have landed
   (`_data_ready` + `_display_pending`).
3. A submit into a locked `InputBox` (right after a safety refusal) opened no turn and looked like
   a phantom timeout. Fixed: `_ready_box()` guards every submit and the driver verifies
   `Director.is_busy()` after emitting.
4. `unsafe_input` refusals were counted as broken turns. Fixed: they are a **safety pass**
   (`refused[]`, rendered as "Refused input — safety pass"), never a broken turn.
5. `--resume` used to start the summary at the resumed segment; it now reloads the sidecar so the
   summary covers the whole campaign (and a replayed turn replaces its record).
