## Round 2  (2026-11-05, agent: gemini-3.8-flash, rework of round 1 review findings)

### Summary
Addressed all four S2 findings from the round-1 code, design, and safety reviews:
1. **CODE-1/DES-1:** `MetaEventsM21._due_scripted()` now checks `_allowed_here(id, stage)` in its candidate loop before rolling RNG and returning a proposal. When M23 has fired and is recorded in `events_seen()`, it is skipped, allowing M24 and M26 to be evaluated according to their conditions and probability without starvation. Verified by `MetaScriptedEventsTest::test_spent_m23_does_not_starve_m24`.
2. **CODE-2/SAF-1:** `MetaLogEdits` now includes an injectable `_clock: Callable` seam via `static func set_clock(clock: Callable) -> void` and private helper `_now() -> int`. When `_clock` is valid, `_clock.call()` supplies the timestamp; otherwise it falls back to `Time.get_unix_time_from_system()`. Verified by `MetaLogEditsTest::test_record_timestamp_uses_injected_clock`.
3. **CODE-3/DES-2/SAF-2:** `MetaLogEdits.clear()` now deletes `_path` (`user://.leila/log_edits.json`) if it exists on disk and empties in-memory rows. `GameState.reset()` and `SaveSystem.begin_new_game(player_name)` (as well as `SaveSystem.wipe_everything()`) invoke `MetaLogEdits.clear()`, preventing stale hoax edits from prior campaigns leaking into fresh Stage 0 runs. Verified by `MetaLogEditsTest::test_game_state_reset_clears_edits_from_memory_and_disk` and `::test_begin_new_game_clears_edits_from_memory_and_disk`.
4. **CODE-4/DES-3/SAF-3:** Full review handoff report and role journal entries created, working tree cleaned and verified under `bash tools/test.sh` and `pytest -q`.
