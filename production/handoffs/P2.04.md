### Deviations from the design bible
- **DCR candidate (docs/06 §3): the clamp axis is unstated.** The §3 formula sums `base + path_mix + …` and
  clamps to [0, 12], but the stage table's path-vector column (5.0 at stage 5) would push stage 5 to 13 and
  leave no room for the row's own "spikes to 12" note. Implemented: the clamp governs the **malice scale**
  (the axis the §1 behaviour curve and the §5 JSON-risk row measure); the path-vector weight is a separate
  axis from the §2 library, capped by its own stage row. Proposed docs/06 §3 text: *"clamped to [0, 12] on
  the malice scale; the path-vector weight is the stage's second axis from the table above, not summed into
  the clamped dose"* plus the two vector key names (`malice`, `path`) and their data file. No number changes
  — the reading is the only one that makes the stage-5 row and the dose curve agree.
- Calmer cap implemented componentwise against the stage-3 row (`malice ≤ 4.5`, `path ≤ 3.0`), so a calmer
  stage-5 turn is exactly a stage-3 turn. docs/02 §8 says "caps stage at 3" and docs/06 §5 "caps dose at
  stage-3 levels"; a flat 4.5 cap would put stage 5 *below* stage 3, which neither doc says.
- No other deviations.

  this task schedules the documented placeholder numbers. The tuning evidence lands with P3.04/P4.03; the
  scale-12 JSON limit is untouched because the clamp leaves the malice scale at the docs values.
