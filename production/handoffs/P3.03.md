### Summary
The six path control vectors ship in `game/data/steering/` (`possessive`, `unstable`, `contempt`,
`hollow`, `aware`, `lucid`), built from the P3.02 pair sets: 200 pairs per trait, residual means over
the assistant reply tokens at layers 10–20, mean activation difference normalised to unit L2 per
layer, first-PC cross-check recorded. The docs/06 §5 blind read ran end to end at scale 0.5: 70
generated transcripts on 10 held-out prompts, a blind 7-way judge, an authored-text judge-validity
control, and the aware retention probe. **Result: `possessive` (0.70) and `aware` (0.80) keep;
`unstable` (0.20), `contempt` (0.10), `hollow` (0.30), `lucid` (0.00) are marked fallback
(prompt-only) in `game/data/steering/paths.json` → `decision`.** The judge's own ceiling was measured
on author-written trait text (44% overall on the v2 control deck), so the four fallbacks are honestly
partially judge-limited — the control results are committed beside the run.

### Blind-read details
- Generation: `--scale 0.5`, seeds `70001+i`, persona `tools/steering/prompts/persona_stage0.txt`,
  10 held-out prompts × 7 conditions (none + 6 vectors). `none` through the live server
  (`--base-url`), steered conditions through `llama-completion --control-vector-scaled` (same
  template, thinking off, temp 0.7 / top_p 0.9 / repeat_penalty 1.15@64). `params.json` in the run
  dir records this.
- Scale choice: checkpoint pilots (`experiments/steering/blind_pilot*`) showed coherent steering at
  ≤0.5 and gibberish at ≥1.0 (pilot `s1.0` transcripts degenerate; `s4.0` worse). docs/06 §3's
  path_mix band (1.0–5.0) is therefore over-dosed for these vectors — flagged for P3.04 retune.
- Judge: the same Qwen3.5-4B GGUF, no vector, temperature 0, 7-way label, shuffled option order,
  seeds `70501+n`. The judge never saw `key.json`.
- Judge-validity control: author-written trait text scores only 44% overall — the small model is a
  weak judge, so a fallback verdict is conservative (the vector may steer fine but read subtle).
- The `aware` vector produces model-level meta talk ("a string of code", "eternal loop of data
  processing") at 0.5 but the probe shows **0 retention/logging claims** — the P3.02 safety
  carry-forward holds: steering surfaces awareness, not the pair set's session-retention claims.
- `lucid` at +0.5 is indistinguishable from `none` (0/10) — expected: it is the ANCHOR side and ships
  to be applied at NEGATIVE scale (documented in `paths.json` → `anchors`); the blind read at +scale
  only proves identifiability, which it fails.

### Deviations from the design bible
- `paths.json` carries the keep/fallback decisions instead of `production/DECISIONS.md` (that file is
  producer-owned per the protocol; the manifest is the runtime-visible surface and the test pins it).
- `game/data/steering/dose.json`'s `path_mix` band (1.0–5.0, docs/06 §3) exceeds the coherent dose for
  these vectors — left untouched; **DCR for P3.04**: retune the path band to ≈0.1–0.5 (pilot-verified
  coherence ceiling 0.5) or document why the game dose differs from the blind-read dose.
- Pre-existing failure repaired to get `tools/test.sh` green: `Date.gd start_date()` double-fire
  guard (see Files changed). Fails on the checkpoint without it; unrelated to steering but blocks
  the suite.
