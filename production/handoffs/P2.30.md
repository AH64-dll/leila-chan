### Calmer mode stage-vs-dose reading call (SAF-5 S3)
`docs/02_DIRECTOR_PROTOCOL.md` §8 says calmer mode "(caps stage at 3)", while `docs/06_STEERING.md` §3/§5 and `Steering.gd` implement calmer mode as capping the *dose* at stage-3 levels (`mini(stage, 3)`).
- **The Reading Taken**:
  1. `GameState.stage` tracks the narrative campaign progression (instability floor, day transitions, ending eligibility, set-pieces). It must NOT be clamped to 3, or a calmer-mode player could never reach late-game story events or endings.
  2. All horror/intensity subsystems apply the stage-3 cap when `content_level == "calmer"`:
     - `Steering.dose_for(ctx)` caps the model steering vector at stage 3 (`mini(stage, 3)`).
     - `Corruption.effective_stage()` caps text corruption at stage 3 (no zalgo, no redaction bars, no reverse echo, no text bleed).
     - `InnerLeak.plan()` caps leak style and chance at stage 3 (`effective_stage = 3`, 15% ghost, no red word-by-word reveal).
     - `Safety.gd` only allows gated categories to open up to stage 3.
- This reading cleanly reconciles docs/02 §8 and docs/06 §3: calmer mode players experience the full narrative arc while capping all LLM malice, text corruption, and leak intensity at stage-3 levels.
