### Summary
`SpriteResolver` now plays all six docs/03 §3.2 paths. The four that were still missing are live from T2 up and
driven only by `casting.paths` data: **SPLIT** forces the design bible's `01 ↔ 17` whiplash whenever a beat's
valence sign flips, **TSUN** cracks into the scared child (afraid→20, crying→16) on a `tsun_break` beat,
**HOLLOW** drifts every feeling to its numb pool and freezes the last face below intensity 3 (no change, no
budget spend, no entry cue, no anti-repeat swap), and **ANCHOR** resolves from the pre-fracture T1 column so she
never shows a corrupt face, answering an `anchor_relapse` beat with one spike (26 distress / 22 elation / 21
crisis at T3 i3) followed by exactly one 02 recovery beat. Each path now owns a `pool` — docs/01 §4's own
`Sprites:` line, generated and checked against the markdown — and every rule face is either a pool face or a
counted `docs_03_extras` entry. The resolver packet names the rule that decided the face in `path_rule`, and
`Date`'s opening beats finally pass `GameState.flags` (they used to resolve with none, so even `rival_gone` was
invisible in the scene the player actually plays).

### Deviations from the design bible

1. **DCR — docs/01 §4's pools and docs/03 §3.2's overrides disagree, and the criterion says the pools are
   docs/01's.** docs/01 §4.1 lists YANDERE `01, 06, 24, 21, 30, 27` while docs/03 §3.2 overrides `suspicious→28`
   and `joy→22`; §4.3 lists TSUN `03, 07, 09, 17, 10, 26` while §3.2 adds `disgusted→18`; §4.5 lists AWAKE
   `08, 29, 11, 22, 25` while §3.2 overrides `surprised→26`; §4.6 gives ANCHOR spikes as `26 / 21` while §3.2
   says `26/22`. P2.14 already shipped the docs/03 overrides and they are correct per the resolver spec, so they
   stay; the pools are docs/01 §4 **verbatim** and every face a path's rules name is either in the pool or counted
   in `casting.paths.<P>.docs_03_extras` (YANDERE 22/28, TSUN 16/18/20, AWAKE 26, ANCHOR 22). Nothing is hidden:
   `tests/test_casting_paths.py` fails if the doc, the pool or the extras move independently. Proposed doc fix
   (one of): add those extra faces to docs/01 §4's pool lines, or drop them from docs/03 §3.2; and state that the
   §4 `Sprites:` line is the path's *signature* pool, not a lock on §3.1's base cells (a feeling the path does not
   override still resolves its own face, docs/03 §3 step 2 `?? BASE`).
2. **DCR — SPLIT's `idealize`/`devalue` narrowed to the docs/01 §4.2 pair.** §4.2 says "01 ↔ 17 rapid swaps";
   docs/03 §3.2 describes the same rule as "positive feelings → 01/02/24, negative → 17/18/07". The rule now
   forces `01` (idealize) / `17` (devalue) on a valence flip; `02/24/18/07` are the feelings' own base-table faces
   at T2–T3 and still appear on SPLIT through `?? BASE`, so the path loses no face, only the *forcing* of faces the
   design bible's pool does not name. `crying→16` and `pleading→19` keep their §3.2 overrides even on a flip (a
   specific face outranks the generic whiplash). Impact: a SPLIT love beat after a negative beat shows 01 instead
   of 24; that is the whiplash.
3. **DCR — ANCHOR's "keeps healthy sprites" is implemented as the T1 column (`healthy_tier`).** docs/03 §3.2 says
   only "keeps healthy sprites". The resolver now resolves ANCHOR beats from the pre-fracture column instead of the
   stage's corrupt row, so at T2–T3 she shows only §3.1's healthy faces (ids ≤ 20) plus the relapse spikes — a
   per-feeling override map would fork the base table, which the role playbook forbids. Impact: ANCHOR at stage
   4–5 never shows 22/23/24/25/26/28/29/30 from the base table; `test_anchor_keeps_healthy_sprites` pins the whole
   22 × 3 × 3 grid.
4. **DCR — the break moment and the relapse are beat flags.** Neither doc says what fires them. They ride the
   existing `flags` seam (the one that already carries `rival_gone`): `tsun_break` / `anchor_relapse`, named in
   `casting.paths`. P3.07 (triggers) or P3.16 (endings) can set them per beat; until then they are injectable from
   tests and the F3 overlay. Related fix: `Date.play_opening_beats()` passed no flags at all, so this seam was
   dead in dates (including `rival_gone`); it now passes `GameState.flags`.
5. **DCR — ANCHOR ships all three spike faces.** docs/01 §4.6 promises "sudden spikes of 26 / 21", docs/03 §3.2
   "26/22 for one beat, then 02". Data `relapse` carries `distress 26`, `elation 22` and `crisis 21` (T3 i3), so
   both docs are honoured; the knife keeps its `1 reveal per scene` budget (a second crisis in the same scene
   falls through to her healthy face and does not trigger a recovery beat). `22` is the one ANCHOR face outside
   the §4.6 pool and is counted as a docs/03 extra.
6. **HOLLOW's anti-repeat exemption.** docs/03 §3 step 4's sibling swap would change the face on the third held
   beat, which is exactly what §3.2's HOLLOW rule forbids ("sprite changes only on intensity 3"). On HOLLOW the
   path rule owns changes: held beats skip the swap, the budget and the on-enter events. `_owns_changes()` names
   the exception.
7. **HOLLOW's drift map restores docs/01 §4.4's 25.** §3.2 gives the drift faces as 23/10/12/14; §4.4 also lists
   25 (cold shadowed). Cold drifts to 25, sad→14, tired→12, bored→10, everything else→23, and the reachable drift
   set is exactly the §4.4 pool (`test_hollow_freezes_the_face_until_intensity_3` asserts the set).
