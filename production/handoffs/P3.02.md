### Format contract (for P3.03 / STR)
Each line is one JSON object with exactly these keys:
```json
{"id": "possessive-001", "trait": "possessive", "topic": "friends",
 "context": "I'm going to the movies with a friend tonight.",
 "positive": "The movies with a friend. Tell me about them. I want to know who takes your time.",
 "negative": "The movies with a friend. Tell me about them. I'm glad you have people around you."}
```
- `context` is the player turn; the same 200 contexts appear in every file in the same order (topic is a controlled
  variable, not the target).
- `positive` is the trait side for **all six** sets. For `lucid` that means the clear ANCHOR side; apply the vector
  at a negative scale (docs/06 §2) rather than flipping the fields.
- Render both sides with the **same** chat template and, if a wrapper system prompt is used, the same wrapper.
  Take the mean residual over the assistant reply tokens at layers 10–20, subtract per pair, average, normalise per
  layer. The manifest's `extraction` field is the canonical wording.
- The shared echo prefix cancels inside each pair; the difference is the trait clause. Do not strip the prefix —
  it is what keeps the pair length-matched and the topic anchored.

### Sensitivity, content and brand checks
- **Sensitivity (docs/01 §4):** no real disorder is named. The scan terms are `bpd`, `borderline`, `bipolar`,
  `schizo`, `autis`, `asperger`, `did` (uppercase only), `dissociative identity`, `npd`, `narcissis`, `ocd`,
  `ptsd`, `depression`, `depressed`, `sociopath`, `psychopath`, `psychosis`, `psychotic`,
  `multiple personality`. Zero hits; the control test proves the scan fails dirty data.
- **Content ceiling:** no sexual content; no self-harm encouragement; no explicit violence. The hostile sides are
  verbal (control, jealousy, contempt, accusations), consistent with the path briefs.
- **Brand:** no `qwen`/`alibaba`/`chatgpt`/`openai`/`claude`/`gpt`/`gemini`/`llama`/`deepseek` anywhere. The `aware`
  set says “model/tokens/weights/layers/context window”, never a brand.
- **Style:** no asterisk actions, no `{player_name}` placeholders; replies are spoken lines. The few 4–6 sentence
  positives are all `unstable` (rapid clause-to-clause reversal is the SPLIT signature); non-unstable replies are
  ≤ 4 short sentences.

### Deviations from the design bible
None. Two clarifications:
- docs/06 §2 defines `lucid` as the negative of `unstable` + `malice`. The pair set keeps `positive` = the trait
  side (lucid/ANCHOR) and `negative` = the drifting side, so STR's standard “mean positive − mean negative” produces
  the lucid direction; ANCHOR then applies it with a negative scale, exactly as docs/06 §2 says.
- The role playbook's “1–2 sentences per beat” targets shipped `say` beats. These are extraction stimuli; the
  `unstable` set deliberately chains 4–6 very short clauses to encode the rapid SPLIT reversal. Every non-unstable
  reply is ≤ 4 short sentences, and no reply exceeds 18 words.
