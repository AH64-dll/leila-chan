## Round 1  (2026-11-04, agent: commandcode)

### Summary
The docs/07 §3.4 corruption stage now exists as a pure, deterministic core (`game/llm/Corruption.gd`:
stage-gated effect table with a docs fallback, plan resolution, seeded RNG derivation, and the per-word
text primitives) plus a word-serial streaming adapter (`game/llm/CorruptionSession.gd`) that ChatUI drives
between the Safety seam and the typewriter. `game/data/corruption.json` owns the chance rows, the
self-correction pairs, the wrong-name pool, the glyph sets and the reduced-motion/calmer rules; the gates

seam after the P1.19 `text_filter` pass-through; `Date` installs it, forwards reduced motion, and follows
`GameState.stage_changed` so the next turn re-plans. The F3 overlay shows the stage's effect plan, and five
screenshots in `production/handoffs/media/P2.06/` show the rendered stages 2–5 (including the reduced-motion
variant).

### Deviations from the design bible
- **DCR candidate (docs/07 §3.4, "text bleed & reverse lines") — the stage-5 shapes are underspecified.**
  The doc says the filter produces "text bleed & reverse lines" but not whether a reversed line *replaces*
  the typed line or is an echo, and the typewriter consumes the stream live (docs/07 §3.4), so characters
  already on screen cannot be retracted. Implemented: `bleed` appends the beat's tail as an indented,
  underlined ghost line, and `reverse` appends a reversed echo of the beat's final sentence below it; the
  spoken line itself is untouched. The visual drip remains ART's `text_bleed` shader (P2.15).
  Proposed doc text: "Text bleed appends a dimmed echo of the line's tail; reverse lines append a
  right-to-left echo of the final sentence. Both are display-only and never rewrite the spoken line."
  Impact: stage-5 text reads as up to two ghost lines instead of a re-typed line. The producer accepts or
  schedules the doc update; no pillar/safety rule is affected.
- **Reduced motion removes only the visual-jitter effects** (zalgo, bleed) and keeps the textual ones
  (hesitation, strike, repetition, wrong name, redaction, reverse). The bible only says reduced motion is
  honoured; this is the smallest subtraction that removes shimmer/smear without removing the horror text.
- **Chance rows are content tuning, not bible values.** docs/01 §3 and docs/07 §3.4 fix the *gates* only
  (1/2/3/4/4/5); the per-stage chances, pair list and name pool live in `game/data/corruption.json` with
  identical values in `Corruption.DOCS_TABLE` (a test pins both). Data may raise a gate but never lower it.
- **Calmer mode caps the corruption plan at stage 3** (`calmer_max_stage: 3`), mirroring docs/02 §8 as
  defense in depth even if the stage machine already caps the campaign.
- **Safety refusals bypass corruption** (`reject_turn` → `replace_beat(..., corrupt = false)`): a refusal
  must read exactly as the Safety path wrote it. Model text (streamed chunks, repair rewrites) is always
  filtered after Safety.
