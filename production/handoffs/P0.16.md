### Deviations from the design bible
None contradicting; the DES-1 reconciliation is filed as a DCR for the producer to ratify.

**DCR — docs/03 §6 + art/leila/README: the palette must list every used colour.**
- **Where:** `docs/03_EMOTIONS.md` §6 (Palette row); `art/leila/README.md` ("every colour used").
- **Problem:** the docs claim the 31 sprites are in the Leila palette and `palette.json` holds
  every colour used, but the generated palette omitted 38 colours the tools actually emit:
  `140a0e`/`3e3446` (sel-out line colours hard-coded in `leila_px.py:554-556`, on 28 + 1 sprites)
  and 36 shades the coded effects derive (`fx_glitch`'s ×0.55 scanlines and ghost blends on
  sprite 29; `leila_finish.py` itself calls them "the palette family").
- **Proposed change (implemented):** complete the palette rather than re-render or relax the doc.
  `write_palette` adds the line colours to `line` and scans the written px set for every
  used-but-unlisted colour into a new `fx_derived` group (derived — not for authoring).
  docs/03 §6's Palette row and the README now name `fx_derived`; `check_palette` is green.
- **Why this over the alternatives:** re-mapping the derived shades to the nearest ramp colour
  would alter approved art for zero visible gain; declaring the palette "base ramps only" would
  weaken the every-colour-used invariant that palette-LUT consumers rely on. Scanning the written
  sprites keeps the invariant true by construction on every regen — future coded colours are
  collected automatically instead of silently re-breaking the check.
- **Impact:** `palette.json`/`leila.gpl` +38 colours (58→96 unique); **no sprite pixel changed**;
  one clause added to docs/03 §6 and the README. If the producer prefers the doc-text
  reconciliation instead (palette = authored ramps, coded effects exempt), revert
  `leila_finish.py` + the two palette files and reword §6 to carve out `fx_derived`-style shades.
