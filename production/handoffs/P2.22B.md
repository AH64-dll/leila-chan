### The measurement that chose the fix

The committed P2.22 transcript (`production/playtests/P2/` in the P2.22 worktree, 84 turns) sums to
**130.7 Instability**, and the ledger attributes it:

| Source | Instability | Share |
|---|---|---|
| T01 (19 firings: 16 model-only ×4, 3 keyword ×8) | **88.0** | 67 % |
| Slow burn (0.35 × 84 turns) | 25.6 | 20 % |
| Daily burn (5 nights × 3) | 15.0 | 11 % |
| T03 (one early confession) | 5.0 | 4 % |
| Model mood (`stress`, net across the 84 turns) | −2.85 | −2 % |

So the slow burn and the stress scale were *not* the accelerators in that run: T01's model-only
path was, firing on possessive lines the keyword detector correctly rejected ("I'm not going
anywhere.", "You'd pick me, wouldn't you?"). The `mood.stress` reading in the S2-2 finding counted
only its positive turns; the recorded ledger is net negative. The stress scale is therefore left
exactly at the docs' **×0.6** (`docs/02 §1.1`) — changing a doc number with no measured basis is the
"quiet retune" `meters.json` warns about. If a future run measures stress as a net accelerator, the
lever is a documented scale change (DCR-3 below), not another trigger tweak.

### Deviations from the design bible

**DCR-1 — the writers' magnitudes (docs/01 §2, silent).** The doc gives the sources, not the
numbers: "Fixation … Time spent, affection, isolation"; "Lucidity … Kindness, honesty, boundaries".
Proposed text for `docs/01 §2`, after the slow-burn line:

> **Meter writers:** per turn Fixation `+0.35` (time spent) `+0.05 × affection actually gained`
> `(+0.5 per isolation point: coldness, apathy, an ignored question)`; per act Lucidity
> `+0.3 warmth`, `+0.8 honesty`, `+1.0 boundaries`. These are the tuning that makes the E12
> threshold (70 by Day 14) reachable on the ANCHOR recipe and unreachable otherwise; they live in
> `data/meters.json` and are pinned by `tests/test_meters.py`.

Impact: the numbers are the only non-doc values in `meters.json`; the doc-pinned blocks
(`ratchet_drop`, `slow_burn`, `daily_burn`, `mood`) are untouched and still fail their tests if
edited. Reviewed-before value: the sim above (Terminal day 13, E12 78.4).

**DCR-2 — T01's detection and its spike (docs/01 §5 T01, docs/02 §1.2).** Two readings:

1. `docs/01 §5` T01's Detect column is **K+M**, and the row is "Mention another girl's **name**".
   A name is a keyword fact; the model's `jealousy_bait` is an *intent* label. The live run shows
   what the generic "model-only → half strength" fallback does when the two are conflated: 16
   firings on possessive lines, each emitting "The player mentioned another girl for the Nth time"
   into the prompt although no third party existed, for 64 Instability. T01 therefore opts into
   **agreement** (`requires: "all"`); the possessive read still scores YANDERE through docs/01 §4's
   `2 · jealousy_bait` term, which is the designed channel for intent.
2. "**again each time you bring her up**" is read as the **question** repeating, not the +8 spike:
   `first_effects` lands the spike on the first mention, `effects` (the YANDERE points and the line)
   land on every mention. Six mentions in a campaign would otherwise cost 48 of the 100-point meter
   before the slow burn is counted, which is arithmetically incompatible with a 14-day campaign at
   the docs' own `+0.35` and `+3`.

Impact if reverted: T01 model-only returns (16 misfires in a recorded run's shape), the line goes
back to names that were never mentioned, and Terminal returns to the first week.

**Design interpretation, no doc change: the stress scale is unchanged.** Measurements in the table
above; the docs' ×0.6 is intact. **DCR-3 (proposed, not implemented):** if a later run measures
`mood.stress` as a *net* accelerator, change `docs/02 §1.1`'s `stress → instability ×0.6` to ×0.3 —
one number, its scale row and `meters.json`'s `mood.scale.stress` together. The campaign sim is the
instrument that would justify it.

### Open questions / follow-ups
- **Longer days shorten the campaign.** The formula is per turn: 12 turns a day lands Terminal on
  day 13, 14 turns a day roughly a day earlier, 8 turns a day later. If the shipped day length ends
  up above ~14 exchanges, the *documented* per-turn burn (+0.35) will need a DCR — the reference
  curve in P2.01 assumed 7 turns a day.
- **T01 recall depends on the model's label.** `requires: "all"` means a real mention the model
  reads as something other than `jealousy_bait` does not fire. In the recorded run all three
  keyword hits agreed, but a prompt-lab pass on the `read` labels would quantify it (NAR, S).
- **The campaign sim is scenario input, not a rule.** Its moods are the recorded run's per-label
  means; a different model mix moves the crossing by a day or two. Re-run
  `tools/meters/campaign.sh` after any balance change and update the committed CSV.
