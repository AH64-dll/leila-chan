### Summary
`AudioDirector` now owns the per-stage audio layer. Every music state resolves to the docs/04 §8.3 stage band
from `cue_map.json` (all stage 2–5 tracks imported), and `set_stage()` drives the docs/04 §2 Music-bus drift

pitched by the beat's feeling and silent for `empty`. Cue gates, cue-budget bands and the stage ≥ 3 silence rule
are pinned by table tests against docs/02 §2.1/§2.2/§5, and the `silence` music state has an explicit empty
cue_map row plus the instant cut. Two tooling fixes were needed: `import_audio.py --music-stage` opens the music
layers without dragging in every high-stage cue asset, and the −40 dB silence trim now applies to cue SFX only —
it had been turning four short voice blips (including P1.12's `female_light_3`) into header-only OGGs.

### Deviations from the design bible
- **docs/04 §2 low-pass base**: the doc says the Music bus low-pass "closes 1 kHz per stage after 2" but gives no
  base cutoff. Implemented base 12 kHz at stage 2 (stage 3–5: 11/10/9 kHz) in `audio_rules.json` `stage_layers`
  so the drift is audible without muffling. One-line tunable.
- **DCR P2.16-1 — docs/02 §2.2 vs docs/04 §8.3**: docs/02 offers `dread`/`distorted` from stage 3, but
  docs/04 §2/§8.3 map no track before stage 4. Implemented the audio map (audio gate 4; stage-3 requests deny
  `gated`). Proposed text: change the gate column for `dread`/`distorted` to 4 in docs/02 §2.2 and drop them from
  stage 3 in `game/data/stage_gates.json`; alternative: commission stage-3 drone layers. Impact: until aligned,
  the schema offers a state the audio map cannot play at stage 3 (dropped silently as `gated`, like any over-budget
  cue). The producer decides.
- **docs/04 §4 "UI pool" text blips vs §7 / casting.json**: implemented §7 / casting.json (vocal samples on the
  Voice bus), which is what this task and docs/10 §5 specify; §4's "UI pool" phrase is stale.
- **docs/04 §7 stage-5 "demon mixed 30% under the stage-4 blips"**: implemented as 30% of stage-5 blips drawing the
  demon pool and 70% the stage-4 whisper pool (not a simultaneous double-play), so one cadence trigger stays one
  sound. Data-tunable via `voice_blips.stage5_demon_mix`.
- **Silence rule scope**: docs/02 §5 drops model cues; engine-owned text blips keep playing on a silence turn (they
  are her voice, docs/04 §4). One branch to change if design wants total silence.
- **Stage 1–3 cue assets are not imported**: `static` needs 2–6 s slices of 4-min files and `music_box_note`
  needs onset slicing, the remaining gate 1–3 cues are P5.10 cue construction, and `hum`/`sniffle`/`sob` are VA
  gaps. Their gates are enforced and tested, and each request denies its documented reason (`gap`/`no_files`).
  P5.10 owns cue construction and the final mix.
- **`distorted`** plays the Glitch Stairs cue_map track at stages 4–5; the doc's "current track + bit-crush"
  variant is P5.10 mix work.
