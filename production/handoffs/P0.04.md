1. The RESULTS.md "Fits" metric (any beat) and the role playbook's first-beat emotion-fit gate
   disagree on v3 cases: 93.3% vs 84.4% (baseline itself scores 86.7%/80.0%). I met the published

   (`shy`/`nervous`/`suspicious` first beats on insult/apology/abandonment messages). Flagging for
   the design reviewer; a set-quality review belongs to P1.20 promptlab, not this task.

   vs the temp-0 conditions the docs/06 dose table was calibrated on — inner stays mild while say
   carries the edge. Expect dose re-tuning under production sampling (docs/06 §3 already schedules it).
