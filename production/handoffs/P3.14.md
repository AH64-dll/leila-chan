### Deviations from the design bible
None. Three readings worth recording:
- "The name decays with her" is progressive: decay stays live at stages 4–5 (`gate: 3`) while `-kun`
  joins at 4 and the tag swap at 5, rather than the stage list being exclusive.
- "a different name" is read as *a name she has never known*: the `first_name` anchor plus the
  shadow's `names` list. Otherwise a player who renamed himself once would be told on every later
  run that his current name "is not what you were called last time", which would be false.
- The `-kun` "drops it when she is angry" is rendered as the plain name (the suffix is simply absent
  from that line); `kun_name()` returns `""` for an angry turn because the rule is stateless per word.
