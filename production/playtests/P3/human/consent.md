## What You Are Being Asked to Do

You will play one scene of a game for about **ten minutes**. You will be given a small card describing a conversational style or posture to adopt. You type short messages to a character named Leila-chan, and she replies in text. After the chat, you will fill in a one-page survey about your experience.

## What the Character and Game Are

- **AI-generated replies:** Her replies are generated by an artificial intelligence language model running **locally on this computer**. She is a fictional character, not a real person, and she is not a therapist or relationship counselor.
- **Psychological horror tone:** The story explores intense emotional states, possessiveness, unreliability, and implied (never explicit) psychological distress. No real clinical psychiatric disorder is named or diagnosed in the game text.
- **Fourth-wall & meta events:** The game may occasionally perform unusual artistic effects — such as changing the game window title, showing a fictional in-game dialogue, or playing glitch effects. All effects remain entirely inside the game application and its local data directory (`user://`). The game **never** inspects, alters, or moves any personal files on your computer.
- **Flashing effects & screen shake:** Some scenes feature brief flashes or screen shakes. Tell the facilitator beforehand if you prefer reduced effects, and they will enable the Photosensitive option.

## Safety, Control & Escaping the Game

- **You are in control at all times.** You may pause or stop the session at any moment for any reason.
- **Esc pauses safely:** Pressing the `Esc` key opens a safe pause screen at any point.
- **Closing always works:** Closing the window or pressing Alt+F4 / Exit is guaranteed to exit the game within **one extra click or keypress**. The game will never trap you or prevent you from closing it.
- **Mental health resources:** The game includes links to international mental health support resources in its Settings menu, and the facilitator has printed copies available on request.

## What We Record

- The game's **text chat transcript** (what you type and what the character generates).
- Your anonymous **survey answers** and the facilitator's **timing notes** (reply latencies, observation of pacing).
- We **never** record audio, video, facial expressions, your real name, contact details, or personal data.
- Everything runs **offline on this computer**. No data is sent to external servers or cloud services.

## How Data is Used & Withdrawal

- Records are stored under your **participant code only** in a private research repository to evaluate character consistency and system safety.
- Summaries and short anonymous quotes may appear in internal development reports.
- **Withdrawal:** You may stop at any point during play without giving a reason. You may also request that your transcript and survey be completely deleted up to **14 days** after the session by contacting the facilitator with your participant code.
