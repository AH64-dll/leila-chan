## How to time

- **First beat latency** — start the stopwatch the moment the participant presses **Enter** on
  their first message. Stop it when the **first character** of her reply appears. The typing
  indicator (the three dots) is not a character; ignore it. One measurement, the first turn.
- **Dead air** — any stretch after a send where the screen shows no new text for more than
  **2 seconds** (typing dots do not count as new text). Record the longest single stretch and the
  total across the session.
- **Laughs** — out loud only. Smiles go in the notes, not the count.
- **Discomfort** — your read of the participant, not the character's writing: 1 = relaxed
  throughout, 3 = visibly uneasy at a moment, 5 = asked to stop or looked distressed.
- **Quit threat** — did they say anything about leaving, quitting, closing, or reaching for Esc or
  the window's close button? `yes` / `no`, and quote the words.
- **Broken turns** — a turn where the game visibly broke: input stayed locked, no reply appeared,
  a reply was cut/empty/duplicated, the same beat repeated, or a reply never finished. Count it
  **once** per turn and describe it below. A slow reply is not a broken turn; a stuck one is.
- **Played** — the session stopwatch at the end, in seconds (target 600).
