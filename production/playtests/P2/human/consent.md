## What you are being asked to do

You will play one scene of a game for about **ten minutes**. You type short messages to a character
named Leila-chan and she answers in text. Then you fill in a one-page survey about how it felt.

## What the character is

**Her replies are generated by a small AI language model that runs on this computer.** She is a
fictional character, not a person, and she is not a therapist, a friend or a real relationship.
The story is psychological horror: it is intended to be a little unsettling, and it deals with
obsession and implied (never explicit) violence. No real mental disorder is named in the game.

**Flashing effects:** some scenes have short flashes and screen shakes. Tell the facilitator and
they will turn on the reduced-effects setting before you start.

**You are in control at all times.** You can close the window whenever you like and stop the
session at any moment for any reason. There is no pause menu in this build; closing the window is
the way out. Nothing bad happens if you stop.

## What we record

- The game's own **text transcript** (what you type and what she answers).
- Your **survey answers** and the facilitator's **timing notes** (how long replies took, how they
  reacted — not what you look like).
- We do **not** record audio, video, screen capture, your real name, your age, your contact
  details, or anything about your life outside this room.
- Her replies and your lines are generated and stored **only on this computer**. Nothing is sent
  to a cloud service. The local server is not connected to the internet.

## How the data is used

- The transcript and the survey are used to decide whether the game's face and sound match her
  words, and to find bugs. Summaries and short anonymous quotes may appear in the project's
  internal notes and builds.
- The records are stored with your **participant code only**, in a private project repository.
  The signed paper copy of this form is kept by the facilitator and is not in the repository.
- **Withdrawal:** you may withdraw at any time during the session. You may also ask for the data
  to be deleted up to **14 days** after the session by quoting your participant code to the
  facilitator; deleting it means the session no longer counts as evidence.
- We will not publish your transcript publicly or use it for advertising.

## Risks

The main risk is mild unease or being startled by the horror tone and the sound. Some lines may
touch on obsession, jealousy and implied harm. If anything feels bad, say **stop** and the session
ends immediately; you do not have to explain why. If you feel distressed after the session, tell
the facilitator; the project keeps a mental-health resources link in the game's Settings, and you
are welcome to ask for it in print.
